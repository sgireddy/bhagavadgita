ॐ Chapter 3 — Karma Yoga ॐ

कर्मयोगः : కర్మయోగము
"The Yoga of Action"  ·  43 verses  ·  You cannot escape action — but you can transform it
Arjuna's natural reaction to Ch 2 is: "If knowledge is supreme, why must I act at all? Why not just renounce?"

Kṛṣṇa's answer in Ch 3 is uncompromising: "No one can remain even for a moment without action." Even breathing, even sitting, even thinking — all are action. The choice is not between acting and not acting; it is between acting selfishly and acting as offering (yajña) — for the welfare of all beings (loka-saṅgraha).

⭐ Essential verses of Chapter 3

BG 3.5 You cannot escape action — even for a moment
न हि कश्चित्क्षणमपि जातु तिष्ठत्यकर्मकृत् ।
कार्यते ह्यवशः कर्म सर्वः प्रकृतिजैर्गुणैः ॥ ३.५ ॥
న హి కశ్చిత్క్షణమపి జాతు తిష్ఠత్యకర్మకృత్ ।
కార్యతే హ్యవశః కర్మ సర్వః ప్రకృతిజైర్గుణైః ॥ ౩.౫ ॥
na hi kaścit kṣaṇam api jātu tiṣṭhaty akarma-kṛt |
kāryate hy avaśaḥ karma sarvaḥ prakṛti-jair guṇaiḥ ||
"No one can remain, even for a moment, without performing action.
All are compelled to act, helplessly, by the qualities born of nature."

BG 3.21 Whatever a great person does, others follow
यद्यदाचरति श्रेष्ठस्तत्तदेवेतरो जनः ।
स यत्प्रमाणं कुरुते लोकस्तदनुवर्तते ॥ ३.२१ ॥
యద్యదాచరతి శ్రేష్ఠస్తత్తదేవేతరో జనః ।
స యత్ప్రమాణం కురుతే లోకస్తదనువర్తతే ॥ ౩.౨౧ ॥
yad yad ācarati śreṣṭhas tat tad evetaro janaḥ |
sa yat pramāṇaṁ kurute lokas tad anuvartate ||
"Whatever a great person does, others follow.
Whatever standard they set, the world follows."
The doctrine of loka-saṅgraha — leadership by example, action for the welfare of the world.
BG 3.35 Better one's own duty imperfectly performed than another's well performed
श्रेयान्स्वधर्मो विगुणः परधर्मात्स्वनुष्ठितात् ।
स्वधर्मे निधनं श्रेयः परधर्मो भयावहः ॥ ३.३५ ॥
శ్రేయాన్స్వధర్మో విగుణః పరధర్మాత్స్వనుష్ఠితాత్ ।
స్వధర్మే నిధనం శ్రేయః పరధర్మో భయావహః ॥ ౩.౩౫ ॥
śreyān sva-dharmo viguṇaḥ para-dharmāt sv-anuṣṭhitāt |
sva-dharme nidhanaṁ śreyaḥ para-dharmo bhayāvahaḥ ||
"Better one's own duty (svadharma), though imperfectly performed,
than another's duty well performed.
Better is death in one's own duty; another's duty is fraught with fear."
This verse is later echoed almost verbatim in 18.47 — bookending the Gītā's teaching on duty.
🌟 Essential take-away of Chapter 3

You cannot escape action — so transform it.
Action done for personal gain binds you. Action done as yajña — as offering, as service, as your duty (svadharma) — liberates you.

Three steps of Karma-Yoga:
1. Recognize that action is unavoidable.
2. Do your own duty, not someone else's, however imperfect.
3. Do it without selfish motive — as service to the whole.
