"The Yoga of Knowledge & Renunciation of Action"  ·  42 verses  ·  Action illumined by wisdom
Chapter 4 introduces the third pillar — jñāna (knowledge) — and shows how it weaves together with karma. Kṛṣṇa reveals that this same teaching is eternal, transmitted from sage to sage in an unbroken lineage. He declares the principle of avatāra — the Divine's repeated descent into the world to restore dharma — and teaches the great paradox: "He who sees inaction in action, and action in inaction — he is the wise one among men."

BG 4.18 The wise see inaction in action and action in inaction
कर्मण्यकर्म यः पश्येदकर्मणि च कर्म यः ।
स बुद्धिमान्मनुष्येषु स युक्तः कृत्स्नकर्मकृत् ॥ ४.१८ ॥
కర్మణ్యకర్మ యః పశ్యేదకర్మణి చ కర్మ యః ।
స బుద్ధిమాన్మనుష్యేషు స యుక్తః కృత్స్నకర్మకృత్ ॥ ౪.౧౮ ॥
karmaṇy akarma yaḥ paśyed akarmaṇi ca karma yaḥ |
sa buddhimān manuṣyeṣu sa yuktaḥ kṛtsna-karma-kṛt ||
"One who sees inaction in action, and action in inaction —
that one is wise among men, established in yoga, doing all action."
The paradox: external work done with inner stillness is true action; external stillness with inner agitation is not.
BG 4.34 The method of learning — approach a teacher with humility and questioning
तद्विद्धि प्रणिपातेन परिप्रश्नेन सेवया ।
उपदेक्ष्यन्ति ते ज्ञानं ज्ञानिनस्तत्त्वदर्शिनः ॥ ४.३४ ॥
తద్విద్ధి ప్రణిపాతేన పరిప్రశ్నేన సేవయా ।
ఉపదేక్ష్యంతి తే జ్ఞానం జ్ఞానినస్తత్త్వదర్శినః ॥ ౪.౩౪ ॥
tad viddhi praṇipātena paripraśnena sevayā |
upadekṣyanti te jñānaṁ jñāninas tattva-darśinaḥ ||
"Know this by approaching the wise with prostration,
with sincere inquiry, and with service.
Those who have seen the truth will instruct you in knowledge."
The classic verse on guru-paramparā — the spiritual relationship.

BG 4.38 Nothing in this world purifies like knowledge
न हि ज्ञानेन सदृशं पवित्रमिह विद्यते ।
तत्स्वयं योगसंसिद्धः कालेनात्मनि विन्दति ॥ ४.३८ ॥
న హి జ్ఞానేన సదృశం పవిత్రమిహ విద్యతే ।
తత్స్వయం యోగసంసిద్ధః కాలేనాత్మని విందతి ॥ ౪.౩౮ ॥
na hi jñānena sadṛśaṁ pavitram iha vidyate |
tat svayaṁ yoga-saṁsiddhaḥ kālenātmani vindati ||
"In this world, there is nothing as purifying as knowledge.
The one perfected in yoga, in time, finds it of himself, in himself."
🌟 Essential take-away of Chapter 4

Action illumined by knowledge does not bind.
Karma-Yoga + Jñāna together: do your work, but with the understanding of who you really are. This is "action in inaction" — outwardly engaged, inwardly free.

The Lord descends (avatāra) again and again to teach this; you can receive it the same way the sages always have: with humility, inquiry, and service (4.34).
