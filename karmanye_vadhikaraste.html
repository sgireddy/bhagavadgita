ॐ Karmaṇy-evādhikāras Te · Yoga-sthaḥ Kuru Karmāṇi ॐ

Bhagavad Gītā · Chapter 2 · Verses 47 & 48 — The heart of Karma-Yoga · Lord Kṛṣṇa to Arjuna
📜 Why these two verses belong together: Verse 2.47 tells you what to renounce — the fruits of action. Verse 2.48 tells you how — by being established in yoga, even-minded toward success and failure. Together they form the Gītā's complete formula for selfless action: act without claim, with equanimity. The closing line of 2.48 — “samatvaṁ yoga ucyate” — gives the Gītā's most famous definition: “evenness of mind is yoga.”
Verse 2.47 — The Principle
कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥
కర్మణ్యేవాధికారస్తే మా ఫలేషు కదాచన ।
మా కర్మఫలహేతుర్భూర్మా తే సంగోऽస్త్వకర్మణి ॥
karmaṇy evādhikāras te mā phaleṣu kadācana
mā karma-phala-hetur bhūr mā te saṅgo’stv akarmaṇi
“You have the right to action alone — never to its fruits. Let not the fruit be your motive, nor cling to inaction.”
Verse 2.48 — The Practice
योगस्थः कुरु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा धनञ्जय ।
सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योः समो भूत्वा समत्वं योग उच्यते ॥
యోగస్థః కురు కర్మాణి సంగం త్యక్త్వా ధనంజయ ।
సిద్ధ్యసిద్ధ్యోః సమో భూత్వా సమత్వం యోగ ఉచ్యతే ॥
yoga-sthaḥ kuru karmāṇi saṅgaṁ tyaktvā dhanañjaya
siddhy-asiddhyoḥ samo bhūtvā samatvaṁ yoga ucyate
“Established in yoga, perform actions abandoning attachment, even-minded in success and failure — such equanimity is called yoga.”

★ samatvaṁ yoga ucyate — “Equanimity itself is called Yoga.”  One of the Gītā's three classical definitions of yoga.
1️⃣ Right to ActionYou may act — that is your domain.
2️⃣ Not to FruitsResults are not yours to claim.
3️⃣ Equanimity in BothSame in success or failure.
4️⃣ This = YogaThat evenness is Yoga itself.
📖 Combined teaching: “Act fully — that is your right. But surrender the fruit. Stay rooted in yoga, abandon attachment, and remain even-minded whether you succeed or fail. Such evenness of mind is what we call Yoga.”  Together, 2.47 and 2.48 are the foundation of Niṣkāma Karma-Yoga — the path of selfless action. 2.47 prescribes the renunciation; 2.48 prescribes the state of being that makes such renunciation possible. Without 2.48, 2.47 sounds like duty without joy; with 2.48, it becomes a complete spiritual technology.
