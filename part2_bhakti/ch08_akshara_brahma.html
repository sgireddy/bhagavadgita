ॐ Chapter 8 — Akṣara-Brahma Yoga ॐ

अक्षरब्रह्मयोगः : అక్షరబ్రహ్మయోగము
"The Yoga of the Imperishable Brahman"  ·  28 verses  ·  What happens at death
Chapter 8 takes up the question that haunts every conscious being: "What happens at death?" Kṛṣṇa's answer is luminous and practical: the state of consciousness at the moment of dying determines the next destination. Therefore the entire life must be a long preparation — a continual remembering of the Divine. This chapter introduces the sacred syllable Oṁ as the consciousness-bridge across the threshold of death.

⭐ Essential verses of Chapter 8

BG 8.5 Whoever remembers Me at the end attains My nature
अन्तकाले च मामेव स्मरन्मुक्त्वा कलेवरम् ।
यः प्रयाति स मद्भावं याति नास्त्यत्र संशयः ॥ ८.५ ॥
అంతకాలే చ మామేవ స్మరన్ముక్త్వా కలేవరమ్ ।
యః ప్రయాతి స మద్భావం యాతి నాస్త్యత్ర సంశయః ॥ ౮.౫ ॥
anta-kāle ca mām eva smaran muktvā kalevaram |
yaḥ prayāti sa mad-bhāvaṁ yāti nāsty atra saṁśayaḥ ||
"Whoever, at the time of death, departs the body remembering Me alone —
he attains My nature. Of this there is no doubt."
BG 8.6 Whatever you remember at the end — that you become
यं यं वापि स्मरन्भावं त्यजत्यन्ते कलेवरम् ।
तं तमेवैति कौन्तेय सदा तद्भावभावितः ॥ ८.६ ॥
యం యం వాపి స్మరన్భావం త్యజత్యంతే కలేవరమ్ ।
తం తమేవైతి కౌంతేయ సదా తద్భావభావితః ॥ ౮.౬ ॥
yaṁ yaṁ vāpi smaran bhāvaṁ tyajaty ante kalevaram |
taṁ tam evaiti kaunteya sadā tad-bhāva-bhāvitaḥ ||
"Whatever state of being one remembers when leaving the body at the end —
that very state one attains, O Kaunteya,
being always absorbed in that state."
The crucial verse: the last thought matters because it is the harvest of all previous thoughts.

BG 8.7 Therefore: remember Me always — and act
तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु मामनुस्मर युध्य च ।
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्मामेवैष्यस्यसंशयम् ॥ ८.७ ॥
తస్మాత్సర్వేషు కాలేషు మామనుస్మర యుధ్య చ ।
మయ్యర్పితమనోబుద్ధిర్మామేవైష్యస్యసంశయమ్ ॥ ౮.౭ ॥
tasmāt sarveṣu kāleṣu mām anusmara yudhya ca |
mayy arpita-mano-buddhir mām evaiṣyasy asaṁśayam ||
"Therefore, at all times remember Me — and fight.
With mind and intellect dedicated to Me,
you shall come to Me alone — without doubt."
The Gītā's most pragmatic counsel: integrate worship into life, not life into worship.
🌟 Essential take-away of Chapter 8

Your last thought is your harvest — so plant accordingly every day.
Death is not a separate event from life; it is the final exam of your daily attention. Whatever you have given your mind to throughout life will spontaneously arise at the end.

The practice: at all times (sarveṣu kāleṣu), in every state of life, keep remembering the Divine — "and fight" means: do not retreat from life. Action and remembrance go together.
