ॐ Chapter 12 — Bhakti Yoga ॐ

भक्तियोगः : భక్తియోగము
"The Yoga of Devotion"  ·  20 verses  ·  The shortest, sweetest chapter — Part II's culmination
After the overwhelming cosmic vision of Ch 11, Arjuna asks a practical question: "Which is better — to worship You as the personal form, or as the formless Absolute?"

Kṛṣṇa's answer is gentle and direct: both reach the same goal, but loving the personal form is easier for embodied beings. The chapter then paints — in just 8 verses (12.13–20) — the most beloved portrait in all the Gītā: the qualities of the devotee dear to the Lord. These verses are recited daily in many traditions as a kind of self-examination mirror.

⭐ Essential verses of Chapter 12

BG 12.8 Fix your mind on Me — and you shall live in Me, without doubt
मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय ।
निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः ॥ १२.८ ॥
మయ్యేవ మన ఆధత్స్వ మయి బుద్ధిం నివేశయ ।
నివసిష్యసి మయ్యేవ అత ఊర్ధ్వం న సంశయః ॥ ౧౨.౮ ॥
mayy eva mana ādhatsva mayi buddhiṁ niveśaya |
nivasiṣyasi mayy eva ata ūrdhvaṁ na saṁśayaḥ ||
"Fix your mind on Me alone, place your intellect in Me —
you shall live in Me hereafter. There is no doubt."
BG 12.13–14 ⭐ The portrait of the devotee dear to the Lord (first verse)
अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः करुण एव च ।
निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी ॥
सन्तुष्टः सततं योगी यतात्मा दृढनिश्चयः ।
मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः ॥
అద్వేష్టా సర్వభూతానాం మైత్రః కరుణ ఏవ చ ।
నిర్మమో నిరహంకారః సమదుఃఖసుఖః క్షమీ ॥
సంతుష్టః సతతం యోగీ యతాత్మా దృఢనిశ్చయః ।
మయ్యర్పితమనోబుద్ధిర్యో మద్భక్తః స మే ప్రియః ॥
advēṣṭā sarva-bhūtānāṁ maitraḥ karuṇa eva ca |
nirmamo nirahaṅkāraḥ sama-duḥkha-sukhaḥ kṣamī ||
santuṣṭaḥ satataṁ yogī yatātmā dṛḍha-niścayaḥ |
mayy arpita-mano-buddhir yo mad-bhaktaḥ sa me priyaḥ ||
"One who is free of hatred toward any being, friendly and compassionate,
without 'mine'-ness, without ego, equal in pain and pleasure, forgiving,
ever content, disciplined, of firm resolve,
with mind and intellect surrendered to Me — that devotee is dear to Me."

BG 12.15 One who disturbs none and is disturbed by none
यस्मान्नोद्विजते लोको लोकान्नोद्विजते च यः ।
हर्षामर्षभयोद्वेगैर्मुक्तो यः स च मे प्रियः ॥ १२.१५ ॥
యస్మాన్నోద్విజతే లోకో లోకాన్నోద్విజతే చ యః ।
హర్షామర్షభయోద్వేగైర్ముక్తో యః స చ మే ప్రియః ॥ ౧౨.౧౫ ॥
yasmān nodvijate loko lokān nodvijate ca yaḥ |
harṣāmarṣa-bhayodvegair mukto yaḥ sa ca me priyaḥ ||
"One whom the world does not trouble, and who does not trouble the world —
free from elation, intolerance, fear, and agitation —
that one too is dear to Me."
🌟 Essential take-away of Chapter 12

Love the Divine in any form — but love truly.
Don't get stuck arguing about whether God has form or is formless. The Lord doesn't mind either way — He minds whether your heart is in it.

And the test of true devotion is not in rituals or recitations but in character:
• friendly to all (maitraḥ)
• compassionate (karuṇaḥ)
• free of "I" and "mine" (nirmamo nirahaṅkāraḥ)
• even-minded in joy & sorrow (sama-duḥkha-sukhaḥ)
• disturbs no one and is disturbed by no one (12.15)

"Such a devotee is dear to Me." — said five times in this single chapter.
