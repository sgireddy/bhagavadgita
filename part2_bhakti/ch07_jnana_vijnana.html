ॐ Chapter 7 — Jñāna-Vijñāna Yoga ॐ

ज्ञानविज्ञानयोगः : జ్ఞానవిజ్ఞానయోగము
"Knowledge & Realization"  ·  30 verses  ·  Part II begins — knowing the Divine
Part II begins with Kṛṣṇa saying: "Of thousands of people, perhaps one strives for perfection; of those who strive, perhaps one knows Me truly" (7.3). He then unveils the full picture of who He is: the source of both the manifest material world (aparā prakṛti) and the unseen spiritual principle (parā prakṛti) — and beyond both, the supreme reality on which all is strung like beads on a thread.

⭐ Essential verses of Chapter 7

BG 7.7 The string on which all beads are strung
मत्तः परतरं नान्यत्किञ्चिदस्ति धनञ्जय ।
मयि सर्वमिदं प्रोतं सूत्रे मणिगणा इव ॥ ७.७ ॥
మత్తః పరతరం నాన్యత్కించిదస్తి ధనంజయ ।
మయి సర్వమిదం ప్రోతం సూత్రే మణిగణా ఇవ ॥ ౭.౭ ॥
mattaḥ parataraṁ nānyat kiñcid asti dhanañjaya |
mayi sarvam idaṁ protaṁ sūtre maṇi-gaṇā iva ||
"There is nothing higher than Me, O Dhanañjaya.
All this is strung on Me — like beads on a thread."

BG 7.16 Four kinds of people approach the Lord
चतुर्विधा भजन्ते मां जनाः सुकृतिनोऽर्जुन ।
आर्तो जिज्ञासुरर्थार्थी ज्ञानी च भरतर्षभ ॥ ७.१६ ॥
చతుర్విధా భజంతే మాం జనాః సుకృతినోఽర్జున ।
ఆర్తో జిజ్ఞాసురర్థార్థీ జ్ఞానీ చ భరతర్షభ ॥ ౭.౧౬ ॥
catur-vidhā bhajante māṁ janāḥ su-kṛtino 'rjuna |
ārto jijñāsur arthārthī jñānī ca bharatarṣabha ||
"Four kinds of virtuous people worship Me, O Arjuna:
the distressed (ārta), the seeker of knowledge (jijñāsu), the seeker of wealth (arthārthī), and the wise (jñānī)."
All four are dear to Him — but the next verse (7.17) says the jñānī is dearest, "for he is my own self."
BG 7.19 After many births, the wise one comes to Me — Vāsudeva is all
बहूनां जन्मनामन्ते ज्ञानवान्मां प्रपद्यते ।
वासुदेवः सर्वमिति स महात्मा सुदुर्लभः ॥ ७.१९ ॥
బహూనాం జన్మనామంతే జ్ఞానవాన్మాం ప్రపద్యతే ।
వాసుదేవః సర్వమితి స మహాత్మా సుదుర్లభః ॥ ౭.౧౯ ॥
bahūnāṁ janmanām ante jñānavān māṁ prapadyate |
vāsudevaḥ sarvam iti sa mahātmā su-durlabhaḥ ||
"After many births, the wise one takes refuge in Me, realizing:
'Vāsudeva is all.'
Such a great soul is very rare."
🌟 Essential take-away of Chapter 7

The Divine is the thread, the world is the beads.
Whatever you see, touch, love — it is the Lord wearing a particular form. After many lifetimes, the wise finally realize: vāsudevaḥ sarvam — "Vāsudeva is everything."

Whatever brings you to the Divine — distress, curiosity, need, or wisdom — is welcomed. Begin where you are.
