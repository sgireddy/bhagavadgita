ॐ Chapter 10 — Vibhūti Yoga ॐ

विभूतियोगः : విభూతియోగము
"The Yoga of Divine Glories"  ·  42 verses  ·  Seeing God in everything excellent
In Chapter 10, the Lord turns His teaching into poetry. Arjuna asks: "Tell me Your divine glories in detail — for I am not satisfied; the more I hear, the more I want to hear." Kṛṣṇa responds with a breathtaking list: wherever you encounter beauty, power, brilliance, or excellence — that is a spark of Me. This chapter is the Gītā's theology of beauty: every excellent thing in the world is a window onto the Divine.

⭐ Essential verses of Chapter 10

BG 10.8 I am the source of everything — the wise worship Me knowing this
अहं सर्वस्य प्रभवो मत्तः सर्वं प्रवर्तते ।
इति मत्वा भजन्ते मां बुधा भावसमन्विताः ॥ १०.८ ॥
అహం సర్వస్య ప్రభవో మత్తః సర్వం ప్రవర్తతే ।
ఇతి మత్వా భజంతే మాం బుధా భావసమన్వితాః ॥ ౧౦.౮ ॥
ahaṁ sarvasya prabhavo mattaḥ sarvaṁ pravartate |
iti matvā bhajante māṁ budhā bhāva-samanvitāḥ ||
"I am the source of all; from Me everything proceeds.
Knowing this, the wise worship Me with all their heart."
BG 10.10 To those who love Me, I give the buddhi-yoga by which they come to Me
तेषां सततयुक्तानां भजतां प्रीतिपूर्वकम् ।
ददामि बुद्धियोगं तं येन मामुपयान्ति ते ॥ १०.१० ॥
తేషాం సతతయుక్తానాం భజతాం ప్రీతిపూర్వకమ్ ।
దదామి బుద్ధియోగం తం యేన మాముపయాంతి తే ॥ ౧౦.౧౦ ॥
teṣāṁ satata-yuktānāṁ bhajatāṁ prīti-pūrvakam |
dadāmi buddhi-yogaṁ taṁ yena mām upayānti te ||
"To those ever-devoted, worshipping Me with love,
I give the yoga of discernment (buddhi-yoga)
by which they come to Me."
The Divine actively responds to sincere love by granting clarity of mind.

BG 10.20 I am the Self seated in the heart of all beings — beginning, middle, end
अहमात्मा गुडाकेश सर्वभूताशयस्थितः ।
अहमादिश्च मध्यं च भूतानामन्त एव च ॥ १०.२० ॥
అహమాత్మా గుడాకేశ సర్వభూతాశయస్థితః ।
అహమాదిశ్చ మధ్యం చ భూతానామంత ఏవ చ ॥ ౧౦.౨౦ ॥
aham ātmā guḍākeśa sarva-bhūtāśaya-sthitaḥ |
aham ādiś ca madhyaṁ ca bhūtānām anta eva ca ||
"I am the Self, O Guḍākeśa,
seated in the heart of all beings.
I am the beginning, the middle, and the end of all that lives."
BG 10.41 Wherever you see glory, beauty, or power — know it as a spark of My splendor
यद्यद्विभूतिमत्सत्त्वं श्रीमदूर्जितमेव वा ।
तत्तदेवावगच्छ त्वं मम तेजोंऽशसम्भवम् ॥ १०.४१ ॥
యద్యద్విభూతిమత్సత్త్వం శ్రీమదూర్జితమేవ వా ।
తత్తదేవావగచ్ఛ త్వం మమ తేజోంఽశసంభవమ్ ॥ ౧౦.౪౧ ॥
yad yad vibhūtimat sattvaṁ śrīmad ūrjitam eva vā |
tat tad evāvagaccha tvaṁ mama tejo 'ṁśa-sambhavam ||
"Whatever in this world is glorious, beautiful, or mighty —
know that it has sprung from a fragment of My splendor."
The Gītā's invitation to see God in every excellence.
🌟 Essential take-away of Chapter 10

The world is shot through with divinity — learn to see it.
Of mountains, He is Meru. Of rivers, the Gaṅgā. Of letters, the syllable A. Of trees, the aśvattha. Of horses, Uccaiḥśravas. Of weapons, the thunderbolt. The list goes on for thirty-five verses — not to exhaust the catalogue, but to train the eye.

"Every excellent thing you have ever loved was already a glimpse of the Divine."
