ॐ Chapter 17 — Śraddhā-Traya Yoga ॐ

श्रद्धात्रयविभागयोगः : శ్రద్ధాత్రయవిభాగయోగము
"The Three Kinds of Faith"  ·  28 verses  ·  You are what you have faith in
Arjuna asks: "What about people who worship without strictly following scripture — what is their state?"

Kṛṣṇa's answer in Ch 17 is profound: every human being has faith of some kind — śraddhā — and faith is not optional. But it has three flavors, mirroring the three guṇas. Your food, your acts of sacrifice, your charity, your austerity — even your speech — all betray what kind of faith you actually live by. The chapter ends by introducing the sacred formula Oṁ Tat Sat — the three-fold name of Brahman that sanctifies any action.

⭐ Essential verses of Chapter 17

BG 17.3 ⭐ "A person is what their faith is"
सत्त्वानुरूपा सर्वस्य श्रद्धा भवति भारत ।
श्रद्धामयोऽयं पुरुषो यो यच्छ्रद्धः स एव सः ॥ १७.३ ॥
సత్త్వానురూపా సర్వస్య శ్రద్ధా భవతి భారత ।
శ్రద్ధామయోఽయం పురుషో యో యచ్ఛ్రద్ధః స ఏవ సః ॥ ౧౭.౩ ॥
sattvānurūpā sarvasya śraddhā bhavati bhārata |
śraddhā-mayo 'yaṁ puruṣo yo yac-chraddhaḥ sa eva saḥ ||
"The faith of each person is according to one's inner nature, O Bhārata.
A human being is made of faith — as is one's faith, so one is."
One of the most psychologically precise verses in Indian literature.

BG 17.20 Sāttvika charity — given without expectation
दातव्यमिति यद्दानं दीयतेऽनुपकारिणे ।
देशे काले च पात्रे च तद्दानं सात्त्विकं स्मृतम् ॥ १७.२० ॥
dātavyam iti yad dānaṁ dīyate 'nupakāriṇe |
deśe kāle ca pātre ca tad dānaṁ sāttvikaṁ smṛtam ||
"Charity that is given as a duty, with no expectation of return,
at the right place, time, and to a worthy recipient —
that charity is known as sāttvika."
BG 17.23 ⭐ Oṁ Tat Sat — the three-fold name of Brahman
ॐतत्सदिति निर्देशो ब्रह्मणस्त्रिविधः स्मृतः ।
ब्राह्मणास्तेन वेदाश्च यज्ञाश्च विहिताः पुरा ॥ १७.२३ ॥
ఓంతత్సదితి నిర్దేశో బ్రహ్మణస్త్రివిధః స్మృతః ।
బ్రాహ్మణాస్తేన వేదాశ్చ యజ్ఞాశ్చ విహితాః పురా ॥ ౧౭.౨౩ ॥
oṁ tat sad iti nirdeśo brahmaṇas tri-vidhaḥ smṛtaḥ |
brāhmaṇās tena vedāś ca yajñāś ca vihitāḥ purā ||
"Oṁ Tat Sat — this three-fold designation of Brahman has been declared.
By this, the brāhmaṇas, the Vedas, and the sacrifices were ordained in ancient times."
These three syllables, prefixed to any action, transform it into a sacred offering.
🌟 Essential take-away of Chapter 17

You are what you have faith in.
Faith isn't optional — even atheism is a kind of faith. The question is only: what is the texture of your faith?

• Sāttvika faith reaches for the Divine, the pure, the eternal.
• Rājasika faith reaches for power, recognition, immediate results.
• Tāmasika faith clings to fear, ignorance, harmful spirits, or self-destruction.

Examine your food, your giving, your daily rituals, your speech — they will tell you which faith you are actually living. To upgrade your life, upgrade your faith.

And prefix every sincere act with Oṁ Tat Sat — it sanctifies the doing.
