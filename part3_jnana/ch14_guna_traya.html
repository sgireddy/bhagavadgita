ॐ Chapter 14 — Guṇa-Traya Yoga ॐ

गुणत्रयविभागयोगः : గుణత్రయవిభాగయోగము
"The Three Guṇas"  ·  27 verses  ·  The three forces that weave all of nature
Chapter 14 introduces the great Sāṅkhya analysis of nature into three guṇas — strands, qualities, modes:
• sattva — purity, light, harmony, knowing
• rajas — activity, passion, restlessness, desire
• tamas — inertia, dullness, darkness, delusion

These three are always mixing in different proportions in every person, every moment, every action. They bind the soul like three different kinds of rope. The goal is not to maximize one but to transcend all three — to become guṇātīta, beyond the guṇas. And the chapter ends with the great promise: steady devotion (bhakti) is what carries you across them.

⭐ Essential verses of Chapter 14

BG 14.5 The three guṇas — born of prakṛti — bind the embodied Self
सत्त्वं रजस्तम इति गुणाः प्रकृतिसम्भवाः ।
निबध्नन्ति महाबाहो देहे देहिनमव्ययम् ॥ १४.५ ॥
సత్త్వం రజస్తమ ఇతి గుణాః ప్రకృతిసంభవాః ।
నిబధ్నంతి మహాబాహో దేహే దేహినమవ్యయమ్ ॥ ౧౪.౫ ॥
sattvaṁ rajas tama iti guṇāḥ prakṛti-sambhavāḥ |
nibadhnanti mahā-bāho dehe dehinam avyayam ||
"Sattva, rajas, and tamas — these three guṇas, born of prakṛti,
bind the imperishable Self in the body, O mighty-armed one."

BG 14.26 ⭐ Bhakti is the path beyond the guṇas
मां च योऽव्यभिचारेण भक्तियोगेन सेवते ।
स गुणान्समतीत्यैतान्ब्रह्मभूयाय कल्पते ॥ १४.२६ ॥
మాం చ యోఽవ్యభిచారేణ భక్తియోగేన సేవతే ।
స గుణాన్సమతీత్యైతాన్బ్రహ్మభూయాయ కల్పతే ॥ ౧౪.౨౬ ॥
māṁ ca yo 'vyabhicāreṇa bhakti-yogena sevate |
sa guṇān samatītyaitān brahma-bhūyāya kalpate ||
"One who serves Me with unwavering bhakti-yoga
transcends these three guṇas
and becomes fit for absorption into Brahman."
The reconciliation of Sāṅkhya analysis with bhakti devotion.
🌟 Essential take-away of Chapter 14

Three forces shape every moment of your life — but you are not them.
Sattva binds you with attachment to happiness and knowledge.
Rajas binds you with attachment to action and craving.
Tamas binds you with sloth, sleep, and confusion.

Even sattva binds — though gently. Liberation is to become aware of the guṇas as they move, recognize "I am not these," and through steady, unwavering devotion (14.26), pass beyond them.

"The guṇas act. The witness watches. Don't confuse the two."
