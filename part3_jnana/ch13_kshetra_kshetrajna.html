ॐ Chapter 13 — Kṣetra-Kṣetrajña Yoga ॐ

क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञविभागयोगः : క్షేత్రక్షేత్రజ్ఞవిభాగయోగము
"The Field & the Knower of the Field"  ·  35 verses  ·  The Gītā's most direct teaching on the Witness Self
Chapter 13 opens Part III with a powerful distinction: the field (kṣetra) — the body and everything that arises in it (mind, intellect, ego, senses, emotions) — and the Knower of the Field (kṣetrajña) — the pure witnessing consciousness that observes all this without itself being any of it.

You are the Knower, not the field. This is the Gītā's most precise non-dual teaching. Everything you can experience — including your own thoughts and emotions — is "the field." What remains, experiencing it all, is what you really are.

⭐ Essential verses of Chapter 13

BG 13.2 ⭐ The Knower of the Field in all fields is the Divine
क्षेत्रज्ञं चापि मां विद्धि सर्वक्षेत्रेषु भारत ।
क्षेत्रक्षेत्रज्ञयोर्ज्ञानं यत्तज्ज्ञानं मतं मम ॥ १३.२ ॥
క్షేత్రజ్ఞం చాపి మాం విద్ధి సర్వక్షేత్రేషు భారత ।
క్షేత్రక్షేత్రజ్ఞయోర్జ్ఞానం యత్తజ్జ్ఞానం మతం మమ ॥ ౧౩.౨ ॥
kṣetrajñaṁ cāpi māṁ viddhi sarva-kṣetreṣu bhārata |
kṣetra-kṣetrajñayor jñānaṁ yat taj jñānaṁ mataṁ mama ||
"Know Me as the Knower of the Field, in all fields, O Bhārata.
Knowledge of the Field and of the Knower of the Field —
that is true Knowledge, in My view."

BG 13.27 The Lord dwells equally in all beings — to see this is to truly see
समं सर्वेषु भूतेषु तिष्ठन्तं परमेश्वरम् ।
विनश्यत्स्वविनश्यन्तं यः पश्यति स पश्यति ॥ १३.२७ ॥
సమం సర్వేషు భూతేషు తిష్ఠంతం పరమేశ్వరమ్ ।
వినశ్యత్స్వవినశ్యంతం యః పశ్యతి స పశ్యతి ॥ ౧౩.౨౭ ॥
samaṁ sarveṣu bhūteṣu tiṣṭhantaṁ parameśvaram |
vinaśyatsv avinaśyantaṁ yaḥ paśyati sa paśyati ||
"One who sees the Supreme Lord
dwelling equally in all beings,
the Imperishable in the perishable —
he truly sees."
🌟 Essential take-away of Chapter 13

You are the witness, not what you witness.
Everything you can call "mine" — body, breath, thoughts, feelings, memories, personality — is part of the field. The one who knows them is you. This insight alone, lived in, brings freedom from suffering: whatever happens in the field, the Knower remains untouched.

And the next step: the same Knower is present in every body. To see this is to see truly (13.27).
